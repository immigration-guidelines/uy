Uruguay - published guidance for the officials who decide migration cases
Checked 2026-11-14/15. Re-run: python3 download_uy.py (from the folder above this one).

Result in one paragraph
Uruguay publishes almost no guidance for the officials who decide migration and asylum
cases. The Dirección Nacional de Migración (DNM, Ministerio del Interior: entry/exit
control, residence permits) and the Comisión de Refugiados (CORE, at the Foreign Ministry)
put out laws and decrees (through IMPO), applicant pages and FAQs, statistics and press
releases, but no manuals, circulars, órdenes de servicio or instructivos for their staff.
The few documents that are addressed to officials are inter-agency protocols: two COVID-era
protocols for the migration control points (2020, 2021) and a 2011 Foreign Ministry /
Inmujeres protocol for consulates on trafficking. In July 2026 the Ministerio del Interior
announced two new "guías de actuación" on trafficking for DNM and identity-card staff; they
are not online. Real guidance published: 3 documents, all of the protocol type, two of them
tied to an emergency that is over.
